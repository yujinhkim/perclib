## Completion criteria

For each source passage, separate definitions, results, conditional inputs, conjectures, heuristics and proof arguments. Record the exact hypotheses and conclusions before assigning or reusing a permanent tag. Reconcile overlapping sources rather than writing duplicate assertions. Preserve source-specific formulations as occurrences of the shared assertion when mathematically justified.

For a formalized assertion, require the compiled manifest association and display the fingerprint-bound correspondence review. A module name, source declaration, article link, or green build alone does not establish prose correspondence. Helpers belong in API documentation; the wiki explains the mathematical assertions they support. Missing associations remain gaps, not evidence that a statement is unformalized.
