## Remaining work

The original forty-two passage groups for Sections 2–4 and twelve added groups for Section 1 form a reading inventory, not a proof-completion count. The bibliography and journal-version comparison remain outstanding. The linked martingale, exploration, transport, volume and invariant-weight lemmas now supply the nondegenerate two-ghost argument with explicitly weaker constants. Endpoint probabilities, exact source constants and the bootstrap remain unfinished. [M9TT](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/524449833aa31807706f353a8a10fdc477927d44/wiki/articles/stopped-exploration.md#L245) uses a vertex transport in place of the source’s oriented-edge intermediate identity. Bernoulli BK arguments here are not automatically valid for dependent random-environment laws after averaging.

The finite-tree proof is enough for the countable percolation inequalities through finite-edge exhaustion, but does not by itself reproduce the source's stronger infinite-graph combinatorial formulation. FIXER’s recorded amplification repair restricts k to positive integers and retains the tail constants 1/9 and 27; the recursive retaining threshold is also corrected. Exact source correspondence, including empty windows and the stronger combinatorial formulation, remains separate from these provisional repairs. No permanent tags are allocated by this map, and no compilation or review status is inferred from its links.
