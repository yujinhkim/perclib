## What remains

The main unsupplied arguments are the conditioned-tree diameter and continuum-tree limit, a general progeny local limit theorem with its support restrictions, spatial Green-function asymptotics under specified walk assumptions, and the self-avoiding-walk/model-specific percolation comparisons. Existing finite combinatorics and Fourier estimates do not establish those results.

The deterministic critical exception, periodic progeny support, near-critical family uniformity and literal-versus-limiting zero field are explicit qualifications already treated in the linked articles. They must remain visible when these source passages are reused. No new assertion tag or mathematical statement is created by this map.
