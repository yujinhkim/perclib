## What remains

The classical endpoint-separation surgery remains separate from the proved deletion and weighted-error estimates. General-action formulations must not silently inherit proofs stated only for countable groups. Model-specific lace-expansion verification and spatial/scaling-limit results are not supplied by this chapter map.

The new reciprocal theorem 6PGV preserves the logarithmic susceptibility upper-bound consequence using the corrected squared numerator, with an adjusted prefactor. This does not validate the false stronger source formula, change its existing tag, or claim a sharp logarithmic exponent. Independent review of the new implication and source correspondence remains outstanding.
