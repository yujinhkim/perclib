## What remains

The marginal interaction-deficit equivalents, continuum kernel and CRT normalization, full spatial mixed-moment theorem, random-measure tightness and model-specific localization remain separate work. The long-range multipoint lower bound is unresolved. The normalized high-dimensional volume chain and the low-dimensional conditional criterion already have detailed treatments; their explicit endpoint, localization and damping hypotheses must remain visible.

Soft cutoffs change shorter bonds as well as long ones. Preserve local domination when differentiating, the order of cutoff/field limits, a > 1 in the fractional-tail transfer, the distinction between vanishing and integrable errors, and the fixed truncation in the mesoscopic differential argument.
