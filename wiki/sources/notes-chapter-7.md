## What remains

The full RSW proof, two-sided arm/two-point comparison, adaptive higher-order probability inequality and model-specific continuum limits remain outstanding. The planar tail optimization is already proved conditionally as HPE6/0RX3, with the spatial exponent range stated. SLE-based triangular-site values require separate literature treatment.

The existing proofs of recursive covariance, sweep comparison and conditional planar gluing cover substantial parts of the chapter. They do not remove the probability inputs. Preserve the continuation-copy distinction, the maximum over matchings, the root factor in the sweep, the difference between logarithmic exponents and uniform power bounds, and the lattice-domain qualification in the concluding conformal formula.
