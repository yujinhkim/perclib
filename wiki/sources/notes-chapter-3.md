## What remains

The general cutoff ansatz still needs quantitative summability and matching hypotheses; directional correlation-length claims need their own proofs. Euclidean IIC constructions, spatial high-dimensional theorems and macroscopic cluster counts require source-specific treatment. Universal tightness targets must not be promoted to theorems from their definitions.

The finite-survival block mean is now proved as 4AVL in the linked mathematical bundle. Its expected-mass conclusion remains distinct from distant conditioning, typical size and concentration. The source tail-sum index, sampled-root convention and integer-quantile convention are recorded explicitly rather than silently inherited.
