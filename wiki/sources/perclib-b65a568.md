The two new files are the finite-quantile repair and the three-point tree-graph interface. All 107 previously inventoried module files are byte-identical to b086702. Their contract summaries are carried forward; this is not a new proof audit of those modules. The detailed tables below cover all 14 public named declarations in the two added files: thirteen theorems and one proposition definition, excluding private helpers and anonymous examples.

Every Lean link pins this merged revision. Article pointers now open the corresponding pages in this snapshot when their earlier source revisions are contained in main. The table records source contracts and relevant exposition, not compiled tag associations or reviewed correspondence. A definition of a proposition is not a proof of that proposition. Formal status remains supplied by the manifest and fingerprint-bound reviews.

## Finite quantiles and the endpoint repair

The observable is the maximum full-cluster intersection with a finite window Λ, with empty maximum zero. Its scale M is the least natural n whose non-strict tail is at most exp(−1). This agrees with the [existing convention for nonempty windows](articles/window-tightness-interfaces.md#section-observables), where a singleton has M=2.

The first six quantile/order declarations make no countability assumption on the ambient vertex type; their source expressions use the law applied to a set. Interpreting arbitrary connectivity events as measurable probabilities still requires the appropriate countability hypothesis. The last five declarations explicitly require finite ambient vertices, so measurability and finite-product continuity are available. A finite window inside an infinite graph is not a substitute: its full-cluster intersections can depend on paths outside the window.

The endpoint argument uses continuity of the tail at M−1, whose value is strictly above the cutoff, together with monotonicity of the quantile. It does not assert two-sided continuity of an integer-valued function. The [provisional source correction](https://perclib-private-preview.tomhutchcroft.chatgpt.site/errata/hutchcroft-hierarchical-2021/#quantile-continuity) and the [existing hierarchical endpoint proof](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/28375dca382dc173d92e4c70c345d71ff694de86/wiki/articles/hierarchical-runaway.md#L52) describe the same mechanism; the latter article revision is unmerged. The [merged quantile package](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/pull/341) supplies the finite-model helper, not the hierarchical squared-quantile bound that must precede the endpoint passage. Applying it to internal finite blocks requires that restriction explicitly. Source-author confirmation of the erratum and reviewed correspondence for 2XJX remain separate.
