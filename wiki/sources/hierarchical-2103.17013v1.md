# Hierarchical two-point reading map: 2103.17013v1

Edition: [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.17013v1), submitted 31 March 2021. Locators use the [18-page PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.17013v1), dated 1 April 2021. Inspected 15 November 2026.

This is a passage inventory and wiki worklist, not a full-paper proof or compiled correspondence certificate. It separates averaged estimates, radial pointwise conversion and the probability inputs. Exact-revision article links preserve the versions inspected for this map; they do not describe the current integration status of those articles. The original benchmark article remains unchanged.

## What this map does not complete

The twenty-six entries include every numbered result and remark in Sections 1–2, plus selected definitions and proof passages. They are not a census of every line, reference or unnumbered assertion. Existing editorial source occurrences still require hypothesis-by-hypothesis reconciliation and fingerprint-bound review. No permanent tags are allocated here.

The finite critical-intensity and boundary-flux wrapper is now supplied by [FF30](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/a2e05f393f9cfca42f600cd4ae7a2a903d3e1c32/wiki/articles/hierarchical-runaway.md#L55). The rooted damping/tightness input is now supplied by [rooted-tail proof](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/cb734591b5cd657331b8cad32af2ca00e498f89a/wiki/articles/finite-set-hyperscaling.md#L73). The probability estimate and volume-exponent argument now have dedicated prose proofs, [UVQ6](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/972c53c5923bb31e42e072d9fa7772f6e639f426/wiki/articles/finite-set-hyperscaling.md#L99) and [7GDE](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/lrp/blob/972c53c5923bb31e42e072d9fa7772f6e639f426/wiki/articles/hierarchical-runaway.md#L143). Fingerprint-bound source correspondence and Lean compilation remain distinct obligations. The six recorded errata now specify the quantile, zero-intensity, truncation, root-membership, coefficient and density-range corrections. Their provisional status and the remaining probability inputs must be retained before claiming source equivalence. The map leaves these tasks visible instead of converting a reading link into a proof-status label.
