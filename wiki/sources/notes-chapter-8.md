## What remains

The one-dimensional marginal transition, critical continuity, full hierarchical volume-tail benchmark, Euclidean reference correspondences and superprocess limits remain separate obligations. Existing normalized high-dimensional moment and tail proofs do not establish every regime in the source tables.

Keep restricted and full-cluster observables distinct. In the hierarchical upper bound, both path pieces are estimated in the larger block; no suffix confinement is assumed. Preserve relative-mean truncation, the integer quantile threshold, kernel/cutoff normalization, averaged-versus-pointwise scope and the distinction between physical and cutoff length scales.
