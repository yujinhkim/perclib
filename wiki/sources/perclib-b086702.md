## Corrections and scope checks

The [source-correction concordance](sources/source-corrections-b20bbd7.md) points to the existing errata and distinguishes proposed fixes from remaining proof obligations. Three new interfaces are particularly relevant: VolumeTails retains the positive starting index in the susceptibility tail sum; RussoFinset and FiniteVolumeRusso retain the closed-edge factor in intensity derivatives; TreeCounting retains the edge/vertex shift in volume thresholds. These are source-level comparison routes, not declarations of reviewed correspondence for an erratum.

The new subcritical hierarchical estimate does not discharge a critical two-point theorem. Local finiteness does not imply finite clusters. Independence of internal-domain events does not give independence of full-cluster intersections. The zero-one law for the existence of an infinite cluster does not make the fixed-root event a zero-one event. The locally finite host assumption in survival semicontinuity cannot be dropped for an arbitrary long-range support.

For each missing mathematical interface, compare exact hypotheses and conclusions with existing assertions before allocating another tag. Compiled associations and fingerprint-bound reviews remain the authority for displayed formal status. Neither this module count nor the presence of a theorem in source supplies that evidence.
