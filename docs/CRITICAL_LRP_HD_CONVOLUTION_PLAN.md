# Source-faithful next step for the open triangle

Source authority: `attachments/i-want-you-to-ljpx/tom1.pdf`, printed HD hypotheses on p. 9, Lemma I.4.3 on p. 35, and its proof ending on p. 41.  This is a source audit and implementation plan, **not a formal result**.

The HD two-point assumptions are inputs of the relevant cases, not consequences of the recently proved convolution tools:

* Case 2: `d>6` and `Pβc(x↔y) ≼ ‖x-y‖^(-d+2)` for `x≠y`.
* Case 3: `d=6`, `α=2`, and `Pβc(x↔y) ≼ (log ‖x-y‖)^(-1) · ‖x-y‖^(-d+2)` for `x≠y`.

The paper writes `∇(0,x)=τ*τ*τ(x)`, where `τ` is the uncut critical two-point function.  In Case 2 it applies the strict estimate twice to obtain `∇(0,x) ≼ ‖x‖^(-d+6)`; the `d>6` condition is then used for logarithmic integrability of the tail supremum.  In Case 3 it first obtains `τ*τ(x) ≼ ‖x‖^(-d+4) (log(1+‖x‖))^(-2)`, then uses the marginal estimate to obtain square-log decay of the open triangle.  The required critical exponent loss in the second convolution is `c ↦ c-1`.

Accordingly, the next honest formal target is the literal threefold convolution and its log-integrable tail under the appropriate stated two-point dominations, rather than a triangular-condition theorem inferred from finite cutoff positivity or a Schur row bound.  These bounds must be converted to the repository's real-valued `twoPointFunction`/`Wt` notation without silently disposing of the nonzero-vertex exception or the small-radius domain.  Any convention on the logarithm near zero must be made explicit; a flat normalized source norm and a raw sup-norm hypothesis must not be identified.  Where an `ENNReal` sum is converted with `toReal`, finiteness has to be proved from the convolution majorants or retained as a premise.
