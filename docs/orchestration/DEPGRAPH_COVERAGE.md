# Proof-source coverage manifest — Critical LRP I

CORPUS: .scratch/source/paper.html | arXiv:2508.18807v1

This is the proposed numbered-environment inventory, not a proof frontier or the complete corpus
inventory. It contains the 75 named statements matching docs/CRITICAL_LRP_COVERAGE.md exactly, plus
23 numbered remarks. Displays, explicit citations/cross-references and proof-paragraph inventories
are still being merged from the source slices. Excluding a remark from this node inventory does not
exclude its mathematical content: reused claims and errata must be recorded on the affected slice.
Dispositions may be refined to address omitted proof substructures; every record remains PROPOSED
until independent review. The old text restitution is not evidence here because its converter loses
content around unescaped '<'; the stable HTML IDs and ranges below identify the unchanged primary
HTML. Exact statement reconstruction uses the lossless restitution and primary PDF.

## Record schema

```text
ITEM: stable-source-identity-and-environment-id
KIND: theorem|proposition|lemma|corollary|claim|definition|display|assumption|external|heading|citation|proof-region
SOURCE: path#stable-anchor@Lx-Ly | external:bib-key#result-Ly | external:bib-key#result
DISPOSITION: NODE i.comma-separated IDs | EXCLUDED
REASON: exact source rationale
COVERAGE_STATUS: PROPOSED|REVIEWED|DISPUTED
EXTRACTOR: agent/session
REVIEWER: different agent/session tag | -
```
