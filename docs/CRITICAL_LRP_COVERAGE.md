## Method and meaning of the statuses

The PDF was independently converted with `pdftotext -layout` and `pdftotext -raw`.
Both agree on 75 numbered statement headings of the target kinds. Lines beginning
with a form feed are included in the enumeration, and eighteen heading-like prose
references were separately discarded. Full statement blocks, not only names, were
used for source anchors. Candidate declarations were checked in the tracked Lean
sources; for PARTIAL rows the remaining carrier restriction or missing premise is
recorded explicitly. The earlier stopped `.ultramath/smwi230hi` exploration was not
used as evidence.

- **FULL** — the valid mathematical statement in the paper is formalized for its
  source domain, under the paper's hypotheses. Natural encodings of measure,
  expectation and real powers are permitted, but not missing substantive inputs.
  Where the proof had to be repaired, or a printed supporting display is false,
  that is called out separately rather than changing the classification silently.
- **PARTIAL** — a real theorem or substantial component is proved, but at least
  one source-domain restriction, missing model input, ansatz-only formulation or
  documented source difference remains. This includes corrected versions of a
  printed equation that is false as written; those are not advertised as verbatim
  copies.
- **NOT FORMALIZED** — no declaration establishing the source result was found.
  Related combinatorics or generic analysis that does not discharge the result is
  mentioned only to explain what is available.

For definitions and named hypotheses, FULL means the actual formal object with the
source's full assumptions is available, not merely that related words occur in
proofs. A definition marked PARTIAL still distinguishes the missing cases or
conditions from the known components.
