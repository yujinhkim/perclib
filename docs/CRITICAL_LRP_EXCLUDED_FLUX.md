# Per-edge closed-coordinate and excluded-flux identities

`Analysis/Cutoff/ExcludedFlux/Defs.lean` defines `pairIncr`, the ordered-pair excluded cluster-volume increment, and `edgeIncr`, its unordered-edge counterpart. `Analysis/Cutoff/ExcludedFlux.lean` is an **intermediate packet**, independently compiled and audited after the second-round D1C submission. It does not mark the full standardized derivative identity solved.

The main results are `lintegral_compl_indicator_mul_of_sdiff_determined` and `one_sub_mul_pivotalFlux_eq`, which absorb a closed-edge probability into the observable evaluated on the closed event; `one_sub_cutProb_eq_exp`, identifying the cutoff derivative's exponential with that probability on support; and `cutEdgeDeriv_mul_pivotalFlux_eq_excl`, the exact support-restricted per-edge standardized excluded identity. `sMomentTrunc_union_edge_sub` proves the underlying cluster-volume increment, including the mirror orientation. `cutEdgeDeriv_mul_pivotalFlux_nonneg` gives its sign under the source's nonpositive slope. `edgeIncr_mk` splits the unordered increment into the two ordered increments and `edgeIncr_loop` proves loops contribute zero.

These results do not extend the coefficient identity off support, do not divide a random cluster expectation by the open probability, and do not claim any interchange with an infinite edge sum. The remaining work is exact and nontrivial: the finite fibre identity between the ordered-pair sum and the image under `s(x,y)`, its cofinality conversion to both `tsum`s, and the monotone `A ↑ V` limit to `PivotFlux.paperSum` and `E1Sign.exclMass`. There is no ODE-consumable `F' = βc|K'|(exclMass).toReal` theorem here and no claimed completion of D1C.
